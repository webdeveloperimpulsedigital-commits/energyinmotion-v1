Overview

We are building India's integrated electric freight ecosystem - combining advanced electric vehicles, battery swapping, charging infrastructure, and intelligent support services. Our solutions help enterprises transition to cleaner transport while improving operational efficiency, reducing total cost of ownership, and minimizing technology risk.

Deployment Roadmap

  • 2025
    Introduce 22 Swapping Stations at major logistics hubs throughout India, each capable of servicing 100 e-trucks per day.
  • 2027
    Target the deployment of 45 additional stations in sectors like cement, steel, and mining, enhancing the overall charging network for electric vehicles.
