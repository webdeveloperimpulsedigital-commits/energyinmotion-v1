The Uptime Advantage

In freight, seven minutes matters.

A truck creates value when it is moving.

Long charging stops can reduce vehicle utilisation and complicate fleet schedules. EIM’s battery-swapping model allows a compatible commercial vehicle to exchange a depleted battery for a charged one in 7 minutes.

That means electrification can be planned around the operating day instead of asking the operating day to work around charging.

Proof in Motion

This is not a future-only proposition.

EIM is already building and operating heavy commercial EV infrastructure in India, with 7 operational swap-and-charge stations supporting its growing network.

1st EV Fast Charger in JNPT

AUG 2024

Inauguration of India's first high-capacity EV Fast Charging infrastructure within the Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT) premises to support heavy-duty fleet operations.

Inaugurated by Shri Unmesh Wagh, Chairman, JNPA

ARAI CMVR Homologation

JUL 2025

CMVR homologation certification from ARAI for India’s first heavy-duty, battery-swappable 55T electric tractor, achieved in a record 7 months.

Awarded by Mr. Regi Mathai, Chairman, ARAI

EV Truck Handover to MICT

AUG 2025

Commercial deployment and handover of the first heavy electric tractor to Mumbai International Cargo Terminal (MICT).

Handed over by Narendra Murkumbi, MD, Energy in Motion

JNPA Fleet Flag-Off

SEP 2025

India’s first fleet of heavy electric trucks with swappable batteries was flagged off at Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority, enabling zero-emission container logistics.

Flagged off by Shri Sarbananda Sonowal, Hon'ble Union Minister

1st Public Swapping Station

OCT 2025

Inauguration of India’s 1st Public Heavy Duty Commercial EV Battery Swapping Cum Charging Station at DICT Delhi-NCR, and Flagging Off of the EIM-Foton ASHWA 55T tractor.

Inaugurated by Shri Nitin Gadkari & Shri H. D. Kumaraswamy

India's Largest Swap Station

MAY 2026

Inauguration of India's largest heavy commercial battery swapping and charging station terminal at JNPA Mumbai, expanding network throughput.

Inaugurated by Shri Gaurav Dayal, IAS, Chairperson, JNPA
