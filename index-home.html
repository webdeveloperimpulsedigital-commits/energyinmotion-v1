EIM Solution

Barrier 01

High Initial Costs

Businesses are discouraged from transitioning to electric fleets due to heavy upfront investment.

Barrier 02

Downtime

Extended charging times lead to lower fleet productivity and slower logistics chains.

Barrier 03

Infrastructure Gap

Insufficient charging infrastructure hinders fleet scalability and cause logistical headaches.

EIM’s package of battery + charging cum swapping services significantly reduces upfront investment.
Our Battery Swapping technology reduces downtime to less than 5 minutes, keeping operations efficient.
Our fleet management software will optimize battery charging & swapping scheduling to run the fleet operations with maximum efficiency.
Additionally, EIM uses 100% Green Energy to ensure truly sustainable fleet operations.

EIM Offerings

  • Customer buys Bare e-tractor (without Battery Pack)
  • EIM provides battery pack on per use or fixed cost basis
  • EIM provides the Charging cum Swapping Infrastructure
  • Fast charging and battery swapping to mitigate long waiting hours for charging
  • EIM will take the responsibility of charging / swapping scheduling
  • Battery warranty & functionality risk on EIM
  • Automated battery pack swap in 5 minutes
