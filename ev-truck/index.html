Milestones & Events

15th August 2024 · JNPT, Mumbai

1st EV Fast Charger in JNPT

Inauguration of India's first high-capacity EV Fast Charging infrastructure within Jawaharlal Nehru Port premises to power heavy-duty fleet operations.

Inaugurated by: Shri Unmesh Wagh (Chairman, JNPA)
July 2025 · ARAI, Pune

ARAI CMVR Homologation

Energy In Motion secured CMVR homologation certification from ARAI for India’s first heavy-duty, battery-swappable 55T electric tractor in record 7 months.

Awarded by: Mr. Regi Mathai (Chairman, ARAI)
1st August 2025 · MICT, Mumbai

First EV Truck Handover to MICT

Commercial fleet handover of heavy electric tractors to Mumbai International Cargo Terminal for port container transportation.

Handed over by: Narendra Murkumbi (MD, Energy in Motion)
25th September 2025 · JNPA

India’s First Fleet of EV Trucks Flagged Off

Flagging off of India’s First Fleet of EV Trucks with Swappable Batteries at JNPA, enabling zero-emission container freight transport.

Flagged off by: Shri Sarbananda Sonowal (Hon'ble Union Minister)
8th October 2025 · Highway Corridor

India’s 1st Public Heavy Duty Swapping Station

Inauguration of India’s 1st Public Heavy Duty Battery Swapping Station and Flagging Off of the EIM-Foton ASHWA Heavy-Duty E-Tractor.

Inaugurated by: Shri Nitin Gadkari (Hon'ble Union Minister)
2nd May 2026 · JNPT, Mumbai

India's Largest Battery Swapping & Charging Station

Inauguration of India's largest heavy-duty commercial battery swapping and charging terminal, maximizing fleet transit speed.

Inaugurated by: Shri Gaurav Dayal, IAS (Chairperson, JNPA)

Narendra Murkumbi

Managing Director & CEO

He is an accomplished Electronics Engineer from Karnataka University and holds an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad (1994). He co-founded Shree Renuka Sugars Ltd. in 1998, which has grown to become one of the largest sugar manufacturers globally. Currently, he serves as the Founder and Vice Chairman of Ravindra Energy Limited, a well-known provider of solar energy solutions.

He has held several notable positions, including Independent Director at ICICI Bank Ltd. (2006-2010) and Board Member of the National Commodity and Derivatives Exchange Limited (NCDEX) (2010-2014). He also served as the President of the Indian Sugar Mills Association during 2010-2011.

His contributions to the agri-business sector have been widely recognized, earning him several accolades, including 'The Entrepreneur of the Year' award at the Economic Times Awards in 2010. Through the Shree Renuka Development Foundation, which he founded, he has established three CBSE-affiliated schools and two ITI colleges in rural Karnataka, India, demonstrating his commitment to education and community development.
