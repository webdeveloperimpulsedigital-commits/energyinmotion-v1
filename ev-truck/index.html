In freight, seven minutes matters.

A truck creates value when it is moving.

Long charging stops can reduce vehicle utilisation and complicate fleet schedules. EIM's battery-swapping model allows a compatible commercial vehicle to exchange a depleted battery for a charged one in 7 minutes.

That means electrification can be planned around the operating day instead of asking the operating day to work around charging.

Less waiting. More time on the road.

JNPA
